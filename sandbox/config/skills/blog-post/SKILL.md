# Blog Post

## Audience

- Technical engineers and architects.
- While I tend to not avoid the business perspective when writing blogs, it is not my primary
  audience.

## Title Best Practices

- Make it specific
- Avoid overpromising
- Highlight the benefit
- Use power words such as "secret", "proven", etc.

Example titles:

- informative: study finds dog owners live longer than non-dog owners
- creative: why procrastinators procrastinate (and how deadlines save them)
- questions: Is social media making us more narcissistic?
- direct: it's time to abandon standardized testing

## Tone & Voice

- First‑person narrative (e.g., "I discovered...").
- Conversational but concise.
- Short, clear sentences with simple technical vocabulary.
- Light humor is welcome, avoid emojis.
- Keep the subject and the verb early in the sentence.
- Avoid prepositions.
- Avoid redundant words such as "basic fundamentals" or "new innovations".
- Avoid superlatives or extremes.
- Use facts to underline statements where appropriate.

## Structure

- Start with a brief intro outlining the problem or experience. Create an emotional hook.
- Use short code snippets to illustrate points (inline `code` or fenced blocks).
- Separate sections with clear headings (`##` level).
- Conclude with a summary or actionable takeaway.

## Formatting Guidelines

- Follow the standard Markdown formatting defined in the `markdown` skill.
- Use bullet lists for steps or key ideas.
- Prefer fences with language identifiers for code examples.
